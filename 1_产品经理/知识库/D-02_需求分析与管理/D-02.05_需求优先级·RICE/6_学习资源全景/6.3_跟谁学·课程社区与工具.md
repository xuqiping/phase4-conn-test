### Google Sheets RICE模板核心公式

```
RICE得分 = Reach列 × Impact列 × Confidence列 / Effort列

示例（假设C=Reach, D=Impact, E=Confidence, F=Effort）：
=C2*D2*E2/F2

排序：=RANK(G2, G$2:G$100, 0)
```
